**Short description:** Research investments with watchlists, portfolio views and evidence.

**Full description:**

M.E.R.I.T. helps authorized users explore investment research, monitor watchlists and portfolios, and review market information in one workspace. Optional brokerage features connect through the supported broker. Sign in with your approved account using a password, Google, or a registered device passkey. An existing account is required. Research content is informational and is not personalized investment advice.

**Release notes:** Initial Android release with private account sign-in and device passkeys.
